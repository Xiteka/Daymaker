# Daymaker logo

The approved mark is the abstract Daymaker concept with the larger four-point sparkle and curved side accents.

- `daymaker-approved-concept.png` preserves the selected concept, generated with the built-in image tool.
- `../src/icons/daymaker-spark.svg` is the clean scalable master, traced from that approved silhouette.
- The 180, 192 and 512 px PNGs are app/icon exports. The maskable export has extra safe-area padding.
- Older icon filenames are retained as aliases of the new mark for existing installs and links.
- `../docs/icons/` mirrors the published assets.

Final concept refinement: enlarge only the detached sparkle and its curved side accents by about 30 percent around its existing center, while preserving the main abstract mark, orange tile, framing and colors.
